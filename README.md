## `hello, world` 👋

I am **Vien**, a BSIT student from Bulacan, Philippines, building at the intersection of **Android systems**, **custom ROM development**, and **thoughtful web experiences**.

I enjoy taking things apart, understanding why they work, and rebuilding them with more personality. On one side, I maintain performance tools and ROM projects for the Infinix SMART 8. On the other, I design interactive websites that make school work feel like something worth exploring.

> **Decision over pressure.**
>
> A small reminder to choose deliberately, even when the loudest option is not the best one.
